The Temporal Equivalence Principle (TEP) proposes that the local rate of proper time is governed by a dynamical scalar field φ=φ̄+M_Plu rather than treated as a fixed geometric parameter. This field couples universally to all matter via the canonical unified metric g̃_μν = -A_loc²(u_grav(x))U_μ U_ν+Aₛₚ²(u_drift(t,x))Π_μν+B(φ)Π_μ^αΠ_ν^β∇_αφ∇_βφ (U_μ≡-∇_μφ̄/√(2X̄), A_loc(u_grav)=e^β_A u_grav, Aₛₚ(u_drift)=e^β_A u_drift=e^-u_drift; Paper 0, §2.2, Step 80). Temporal Shear is pinned where the ambient time-gradient is steep — weak-field language: g ≫ gₜ ≈ cH₀/2 — by the environmental-state operator 𝒮_Σ(ℰ). The canonical pairwise benchmark gives an Earth–Moon response of order 10⁻¹⁴ and does not account for the reported range residual as a differential-fall signal; the analysis instead targets the synodic term in the post-fit residual channel, δ r = 13η_residcos D, where D is the Moon-Sun elongation angle and η_resid is the residual-channel amplitude (an Observable Response Coefficient, distinct from the standard metric Nordtvedt parameter η). Evaluating both the Cassini Shapiro delay and the Earth–Moon laser path on the single solved two-branch solar profile (k/√(2))q y²+q² y³=1 (k=16.03, gₜ=cH₀/2; Step 083) gives y_tb(1.6 R_⊙)=2.16×10⁻⁸ (γ_PPN-1=-4.32×10⁻⁸, passing Cassini) and y_tb(1 AU)=1.47×10⁻⁵, which suppresses the direct scalar optical excursion across the Earth–Moon baseline to δ u_⊙^tb=7.43×10⁻¹⁶cos D (δ rₚₐₜₕ^tb=-1.43×10⁻⁴ mm, η_tb=-1.10×10⁻⁸), 3.4×10⁴ times below the measured post-fit INPOP19a amplitude η_resid = -(3.91±0.56)×10⁻⁴ (-5.08±0.73 mm) and consistent with direct-fit LLR bounds. Because at a fixed cosmic epoch inside the screened Solar System (Aₛₚ(t₀)=1) TEP's spatial 3-metric γ̃_ij=γ_ij=(1-2Φ_N/c²)δ_ij is identical to GR's, the unscreened solar Newtonian potential differential ΔΦ_N,⊙/c²=2.54×10⁻¹¹cos D (which defines an unscreened reference ceiling -1/2 L δφ_effcos D=-4.85 mm cos D, η_ceiling=-3.73×10⁻⁴ at T_Moon=0.9969; Paper 0 Step 76, Step 083) cancels in the BCRS→GCRS reduction identically to GR and would give γ_PPN-1=-2/3 at Cassini if attributed to an unscreened solar scalar field; the measured η_resid is therefore reported as an empirical post-fit residual-channel detection whose dynamical or ephemeris-reduction transfer map awaits a source-level integrator refit.

This work analyses 26,207 raw Lunar Laser Ranging O–C residuals from the public INPOP19a ephemeris archives (Paris Observatory, Geoazur), comprising N = 25,445 measurements from five international stations (1984–2019) after standard 6σ MAD outlier cleaning. The primary estimand is a precision-weighted synodic regression on the post-fit residual channel, with inverse station-variance weighting and a full systematic model with annual, monthly, and thermal cos(2D) regressors and a common intercept.

The primary precision-weighted residual-channel estimate is η_resid = -3.91 × 10⁻⁴ ± 5.63 × 10⁻⁵ (nominal WLS 6.94σ; 6.78σ cluster-robust; wild cluster bootstrap 95% CI [-5.37, -2.73]× 10⁻⁴ across the G=5 station clusters). The amplitude stabilises in the band -3.2 to -4.1 × 10⁻⁴ across the estimator hierarchy, from cosD-only (5.25σ) through full-systematic OLS (6.17σ) to precision-weighted (6.94σ). Robustness checks — common-η_resid mixed model, leave-one-station-out meta-analysis, wild cluster bootstrap, phase-locked differential, cross-ephemeris validation on cleaned DE430 (η_resid = -7.04 × 10⁻⁴, 5.96σ; confirming the sign but differing in amplitude by 2.77σ from INPOP19a in the matched window), parametric GR-null bootstrap, and a frequency null scan — all support residual-channel survival of the synodic component (Section 4). If interpreted within the static Parametrized Post-Newtonian framework — an interpretation the residual-channel estimand does not yet license (Section 4.14) — the measured amplitude would correspond to β_PPN = 0.999902 ± 1.07 × 10⁻⁵ from the joint (β_PPN, γ) contour; this mapping is reported for scale only and is not advanced as a PPN measurement pending the integrator-level refit.

The result is therefore framed as a high-significance residual-channel candidate with a TEP interpretation, not as a completed replacement for direct-fit LLR bounds. The 6.94σ figure is a detection of a synodic pattern in post-fit residuals; it is not a measurement of the Nordtvedt parameter, and the quantitative relationship between η_resid and the direct-fit η is not yet established. Source-level numerical refits of the INPOP or DE430 integrators with η_resid left free remain the critical open closure test. Ephemeris-absorption stress tests bound the residual-channel survival amplitude but do not replace a full dynamical integrator-level confirmation.
