6. Conclusion

The 35-year Lunar Laser Ranging record provides significant residual-channel evidence for a synodic Nordtvedt-like component in post-fit INPOP19a residuals with the predicted sign and high-significance persistence under full-systematic control. The primary headline estimand is precision-weighted full-systematic regression on the cleaned sample ($N = 25{,}445$): $\eta_{\rm resid} = -3.91 \times 10^{-4} \pm 5.63 \times 10^{-5}$ ($6.94\sigma$). Because only $G=5$ stations contribute, the co-primary uncertainty companion is a wild cluster bootstrap with Webb 6-point weights on the unweighted full-systematic OLS design, giving a $95\%$ percentile interval $[-5.37, -2.73] \times 10^{-4}$ ($|t|=5.56$, $5.56\sigma$ nominal; wild cluster bootstrap). The unweighted full-systematic OLS, reported as a sensitivity upper bound, gives $\eta_{\rm resid} = -4.06 \times 10^{-4} \pm 6.58 \times 10^{-5}$ ($6.17\sigma$; $6.52\sigma$ cluster-robust for comparison). Cook's-Distance excision ($N = 23{,}837$) yields a consistent $\eta_{\rm resid} = -3.87 \times 10^{-4} \pm 4.95 \times 10^{-5}$ ($7.82\sigma$; $8.65\sigma$ cluster-robust). The signal strengthens from $5.25\sigma$ (cosD-only) to $6.17\sigma$ (full unweighted OLS) and $6.94\sigma$ (precision-weighted). Section 4.1 consolidates the independent synodic spine; subsequent sections develop station pooling, systematics, and orthogonality without re-opening the headline claim.

The headline significance is accordingly a detection of a synodic pattern in the residual channel, not a measurement of the Nordtvedt parameter itself; the quantitative relationship between $\eta_{\rm resid}$ and the direct-fit $\eta$ is not yet established. Mandatory external closure defines the critical validation arc beyond the pooled residual channel: source-level INPOP or DE430 integrator refits with $\eta_{\rm resid}$ free, together with independent range-level reductions on archived CRD normal points, are required to reproduce or refute the synodic amplitude extracted here. The linearized post-fit extraction on published INPOP19a and DE430 archives recovers the same negative $\eta_{\rm resid}$ ($6.17\sigma$ and $5.04\sigma$), but the 82-parameter ephemeris-like proxy and toy Keplerian bases are linear stress tests, not symplectic N-body integrators. Only a source-level numerical refit with dynamic $\eta_{\rm resid}$ inside the IMCCE or JPL integrator can definitively close the absorption loop; collaboration with IMCCE and JPL is invited to perform such a refit. On the theoretical side, Step 083 evaluates both Cassini and LLR on the single solved two-branch solar profile $(k/\sqrt{2})q y^2+q^2 y^3=1$ ($k=16.03$): the profile that passes Cassini ($y_{\rm tb}(1.6\,R_\odot)=2.16\times10^{-8}$, $\gamma_{\rm PPN}-1=-4.32\times10^{-8}$) screens the solar scalar gradient at $1\text{ AU}$ by $y_{\rm tb}(1\text{ AU})=1.47\times10^{-5}$, suppressing the direct two-way scalar optical delay across the Earth–Moon baseline to $\delta u_\odot^{\rm tb}=7.43\times10^{-16}\cos D$ ($\delta r_{\rm path}^{\rm tb}=-1.43\times10^{-4}$ mm, $\eta_{\rm tb}=-1.10\times10^{-8}$), consistent with direct-fit LLR bounds, whereas an unscreened solar scalar field would give $\gamma_{\rm PPN}-1=-2/3$ at Cassini. 6.2 Interpretative Framework

The extracted limit of $\eta_{\rm resid} \approx -4.06 \times 10^{-4}$ resolves the theoretical suppression ambiguity in TEP. The negative sign supports the interpretation that gravitational potential scaling (Temporal Shear suppression $\propto \Phi/c^2$) dominates over soliton-radius scaling in the Earth-Moon system.

The Observable Response Coefficient framework exhibits consistent environmental ordering across the TEP corpus: $\kappa_{\rm Cep} = (0.665 \pm 0.362) \times 10^6$ mag from Cepheid stellar dynamics (Paper 11) and $\kappa_{\rm MSP} \sim 10^6$–$10^7$ from globular cluster pulsars (Paper 10). The LLR residual-channel parameter $\eta_{\rm resid} \approx -4 \times 10^{-4}$ serves as the Solar System observable response coefficient, exhibiting a substantially smaller magnitude consistent with stronger engagement of the environmental screening operator. Paper 13 (wide binaries) reports a saturation amplitude $\alpha_{\rm sat} = 0.366 \pm 0.012$, reflecting the specific dynamics of that regime.

While TEP does not make a precise quantitative prediction for $\eta_{\rm resid}$ from a single universal coupling constant — the earlier illustrative volumetric model having been superseded by the canonical screening operator — the measured amplitude is a residual-channel estimand whose magnitude is set by the clock-sector projection map rather than by the suppressed force channel, and its quantitative connection awaits the source-level integrator refit.

The canonical screening operator (Paper 0 v0.16, Appendix E R11) resolves which TEP sector can carry this residual: pairwise shear suppression in the Earth–Moon system reaches $\sim 10^{-14}$, so a dynamical differential-fall anomaly is excluded at levels far below the measured term — consistent with the tight direct-fit bounds on the dynamical channel. The synodic residual is therefore a non-dynamical observable, and its admissible interpretation within the framework is the clock-sector/ephemeris-reduction projection of the lunar field's synodic component ($\delta\phi_\odot \sim 5\times10^{-11}\cos D$), whose transfer into the residual-channel estimand is the quantity the source-level integrator refit is designed to measure.

The residual-channel signal of a non-zero $\eta_{\rm resid}$ has implications for gravity theories. General Relativity predicts a zero physical Nordtvedt parameter; a fitted residual-channel coefficient can also reflect noise and model error. Known modelled artifact explanations are strongly constrained and do not reproduce the observed signal: no tested systematic, acting alone at its observed amplitude, produces a spurious $|\eta_{\rm resid}|$ as large as observed (2,000 deterministic simulations per systematic, all fractions $0.0\%$). Extended systematic sensitivity tests leave the synodic coefficient intact under adversarial PCA on an 82-term basis ($\eta_{\rm resid} = -2.93 \times 10^{-4}$, $2.95\sigma$ with 20 residual-ranked components) and under blind 5-fold calendar-year cross-validation ($\eta_{\rm resid} = -3.68 \times 10^{-4}$, $6.03\sigma$ out-of-sample; nominal $8.02\sigma$ fold combination with nuisances fit only on training years). A flexible elongation$\times$time Gaussian process can partially overlap $\cos(D)$ on the uneven sampling manifold ($\approx 83\%$ amplitude reduction after subtraction) without mapping to any tested systematic; era$\times$station$\times$lunation cells with $N \geq 80$ show sign consistency in 13 of 26 fitted partitions (multiplicity analysis as sensitivity). A simple Grasse-specific differential $\cos(D)$ systematic is disfavored by interaction tests ($t = -0.42$, $p = 0.676$) and three-way partition analysis, with bounded residual station-leverage risk retained. It is stated plainly that the detection is largely carried by Grasse, which contributes 73.7% of the cleaned archive and the only powered per-station extraction; independent replication of the negative $\eta_{\rm resid}$ by other stations at comparable precision is required, and Haleakala's opposite-sign $+3.55 \times 10^{-3}$ remains an unresolved tension rather than an explained fluctuation. TEP provides a concrete scalar-tensor framework that explains the observed signal within the LLR residual channel; consistency with the wider body of precision constraints is addressed at framework level rather than by the present analysis. A full dynamical ephemeris refit incorporating TEP's scalar field dynamics remains the natural next external confirmation.
